Mi Crucigrama: Crossword Maker

Crosswords with your own words, photos and voice, to give to anyone you like.

Download on the App Store

How it works

1. WriteThe words and their clues. Each clue can have a photo or a voice note. Other people can send you their words from their own phone.
2. Create itCheck for free how many words fit. When you create it, the app builds the grid and, if you like, a word search too.
3. Send itAs a file by WhatsApp, email or AirDrop, with a QR code, or as a PDF to print. Whoever receives it plays for free.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the app’s language?

In the app, tap the gear at the top right (Settings) › “App language” › “Language” and choose one. Then close the app and open it again: it will be in that language. “Automatic” uses your iPhone or iPad’s language. This changes the buttons and menus; each crossword’s language is chosen when you create it, in “Crossword details”.

Does the person who receives it have to pay?

No. Playing is always free, with no accounts or ads. They just need the app on their iPhone or iPad, or the printed PDF.

How do they open the crossword?

If the file arrives by WhatsApp or email, they tap it and choose “Mi Crucigrama”. Opening the file needs no internet: it has everything inside, photos and voice included. If it’s a QR code, they scan it with the iPhone or iPad camera, or with “Scan a QR code” inside the app.

What’s the difference between the two QR codes?

The “QR code with the crossword inside” needs no internet to open, because the crossword is inside the code itself, but it has no photos or voice. The “QR code with everything” includes photos and voice and needs internet to open: the crossword is downloaded encrypted from iCloud, where it is stored for 3 months, and can only be opened with that code. After that, you can reactivate it from the app, on the device where you made it.

Can I correct it after creating it?

With “One crossword”, for 48 hours after the purchase; then it’s locked, but you can always send it and export the PDF. With “Unlimited crosswords”, as many as you like and always editable. If you change it after sending it, send it again.

Which languages can a crossword be in?

Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German or Italian. You choose it when creating it (by default, your device’s language), and whoever plays it gets the keyboard of that language automatically.

What is the word search?

An option when creating the crossword: a word search is made with the same words. The person who receives it can play it from “Play the word search”, and it’s also in the PDF.

I’ve changed iPhone or iPad. How do I get my purchases back?

“Unlimited crosswords” is restored from Settings › Restore purchases with the same Apple ID (also if it’s shared with you through Family Sharing). Single purchases belong to each crossword. Your crosswords are stored on the device and in its iCloud backup: when you move the backup to your new device, they come back with it.

The file won’t open

Make sure you have the latest version of the app. The file is protected: if anyone modifies it, it stops opening. Ask the sender to send it again from the app.
